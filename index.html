Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026
Publisher: Richelle Hernandez Onda / Onda Interactive Studios
Support: support@onda.is · onda.is

The short version

  • No account is required. There is no sign-in and no cloud save.
  • Your progress is saved on your device only.
  • Optional ads are provided by Google AdMob.
  • Optional purchases go through the Apple App Store or Google Play — we never see your card details.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Who we are

Turneeps is made by Onda Interactive Studios. The data controller is Richelle Hernandez Onda, publishing as Onda Interactive Studios.

Questions about privacy or support: support@onda.is.

Game progress

Progress, hint and booster balances, settings and a local purchase record are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this copy. Device backups (for example iCloud or Google backup) are handled by Apple or Google under your account settings; we cannot access them.

Ads (Google AdMob) and consent

Turneeps may show:

  • Rewarded videos, only when you tap a control to earn a reward (for example a hint or booster).
  • Interstitial ads between levels, subject to in-game limits. Players who buy Remove Ads or the Supporter pack do not see interstitial ad breaks. Rewarded ads remain available because you choose them.

There are no banner ads in the current build. Ads are never shown in the middle of a puzzle.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks Google whether a consent message is needed and shows Google’s consent form. On iPhone and iPad, the app may also show Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt before accessing the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).

According to Google, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google data such as device advertising identifiers (including IDFA when allowed, or the Android advertising ID), IP address (which may estimate general location), app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. That data goes to Google, not to a Turneeps server. We see summary reports from AdMob that do not identify you personally.

Your choices include changing consent where offered, refusing or later turning off tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, resetting or deleting your advertising ID on Android, or never tapping a rewarded-ad button. Buying Remove Ads turns off interstitial ad breaks.

Learn more: Google partner sites and Google advertising.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Apple or Google under their own terms and privacy policies. When you buy something, the store tells the app which product you bought so it can deliver and restore entitlements. The game keeps a small purchase record on your device for that purpose. Google and Apple also make limited order information available to us as the developer for accounting, refunds and support. We never receive your card number or other payment details.

Analytics and accounts

Turneeps does not use a separate analytics product (such as Firebase Analytics) and does not offer accounts or cloud save in the current version.

Children

Turneeps is a general-audience puzzle game. It has no chat, no user-generated content and no way to contact other players. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and think your child has given us personal information, email support@onda.is and we will delete it.

The current AdMob setup is intended for a general audience, not Apple’s Kids category or Google’s Designed for Families programme. Those programmes would require different ad and tracking settings.

Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Apart from Apple, Google Play and Google AdMob as described above, the app does not share data with other parties.

How long data is kept

  • On-device progress: until you clear app data or uninstall.
  • Emails you send us: only as long as needed to help you.
  • Data held by Apple, Google Play and AdMob: under their own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold, to get a copy, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to how it is used. You can withdraw ad consent where offered. Email support@onda.is with any request; we will reply as soon as we can, and within one month where the law requires it. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for important changes, mention it in the store listing.
